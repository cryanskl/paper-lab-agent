Physics-Informed Surrogate Models for Engineering Simulation

We propose a physics-informed neural surrogate constrained by conservation laws and boundary conditions, targeting real-time engineering simulation. The method couples a residual loss with a data term so that the network cannot drift away from the governing equations.

Method

The surrogate takes coordinates and boundary-condition parameters as input and predicts the field of interest. A conservation residual is evaluated on a fixed set of collocation points; inlet and outlet boundary conditions are imposed as hard constraints. Training minimizes a weighted sum of the residual and a small labeled dataset.

Experiments

We evaluate the surrogate on a 2D flow problem at Reynolds number 100. The surrogate matches the high-fidelity solver within three percent relative error on average. Boundary-condition transfer holds across the parameter sweep without re-training.

Conclusion

The physics-informed surrogate is a viable replacement for the high-fidelity solver in the inner loop of an optimization. Future work will extend the approach to three-dimensional problems and to higher Reynolds numbers.
